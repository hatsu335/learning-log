### [外部結合]
**軸となるTABLEはすべて抽出されて、もう一方は関連するものだけ抽出される**

**テーブル**
```Powershell
posts table
+----+---------+
| id | message |
+----+---------+
|  1 | post-1  |
|  2 | post-2  |
|  3 | post-3  |
+----+---------+

comments table
+----+---------+-------------+
| id | post_id | comment     |
+----+---------+-------------+
|  1 |       1 | comment-1-1 |
|  2 |       1 | comment-1-2 |
|  3 |       3 | comment-3-1 |
|  4 |       4 | comment-4-1 |
+----+---------+-------------+
```
**SQLコード**
```MySQL
SELECT
  *
FROM
  posts
LEFT OUTER JOIN  - OUTERは省略可能、右基準にする場合はRIGHT
  comments
ON
  posts.id = comments.post_id;
```
**結果**
```Powershell
left join
+----+---------+------+---------+-------------+
| id | message | id   | post_id | comment     |
+----+---------+------+---------+-------------+
|  1 | post-1  |    2 |       1 | comment-1-2 |
|  1 | post-1  |    1 |       1 | comment-1-1 |
|  2 | post-2  | NULL |    NULL | NULL        |
|  3 | post-3  |    3 |       3 | comment-3-1 |
+----+---------+------+---------+-------------+

right join
+------+---------+----+---------+-------------+
| id   | message | id | post_id | comment     |
+------+---------+----+---------+-------------+
|    1 | post-1  |  1 |       1 | comment-1-1 |
|    1 | post-1  |  2 |       1 | comment-1-2 |
|    3 | post-3  |  3 |       3 | comment-3-1 |
| NULL | NULL    |  4 |       4 | comment-4-1 |
+------+---------+----+---------+-------------+
```
### [内部結合]
**双方のテーブルに関連するものだけ抽出される**

**SQLコード**
```MySQL
SELECT
  *
FROM
  posts
JOIN
  comments
ON
  posts.id = comments.post_id;
```
**結果**
```Powershell
+----+---------+----+---------+-------------+
| id | message | id | post_id | comment     |
+----+---------+----+---------+-------------+
|  1 | post-1  |  1 |       1 | comment-1-1 |
|  1 | post-1  |  2 |       1 | comment-1-2 |
|  3 | post-3  |  3 |       3 | comment-3-1 |
+----+---------+----+---------+-------------+
```
